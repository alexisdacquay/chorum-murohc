## Operating rules for the successor

- The main session is the orchestrator. It launches PM, Engineer, and QA as
  separate phases and does not silently combine their responsibilities.
- PM grooms before implementation. Engineer does not close the issue. QA does
  not fix defects. Merge and close only after QA PASS, including fresh
  post-merge verification.
- Read `AGENTS.md`, `_docs/process.md`, `_docs/task-dependencies.md`, the
  applicable role file, testing guidelines, and design-system guidance before
  acting.
- Give every write-capable worker a fresh isolated worktree and neutral
  `feature/...` or `maintenance/...` branch. Never add OpenAI, Codex, or other
  AI-provider branding to repository text, branches, commits, code, or tooling.
- Use GitHub CLI or API. Do not switch to a browser or display an account/login
  prompt without explicit user direction.
- Never read, source, print, copy, or expose `.env` or any secret value.
- Standing authority covers reversible task-local development: isolated
  worktrees, branches, approved locked installs, tests, generated test-only
  credentials, isolated test databases, task services, and cleanup. Do not ask
  the user for permission for those actions.
- Exact dependency proposals still require a record in
  `_docs/dependency-approvals.md` before addition.
- Ask one clear question at a time only for a genuine product, security, rights,
  paid/public-service, production, or material-scope decision required by the
  documented gate.
- Keep progress interruptible and record every material checkpoint durably.
- On a credit, quota, rate-limit, or capacity failure, save the checkpoint,
  retry first after about 30 minutes, then approximately hourly. Do not retry
  every minute or exhaust the retry allowance into `409 retry limit reached`.
- Give compact cyberpunk-style progress updates showing completed count and
  percentage, active issue and role, current gate, next action, and whether work
  is active or waiting.
